Brisbane business guide

Start Your Own Business in Brisbane

From a first idea to opening day: follow these practical steps and use the official government services linked below.

01Research your idea

Identify your customers, competitors, expected costs and likely demand. Test the idea and prepare a straightforward business plan and budget.

Business Queensland: Starting a business

02Choose a business structure

Decide whether to operate as a sole trader, partnership, company or trust. The structure affects your legal, tax and reporting responsibilities. Get professional advice where needed.

Australian Government: Guide to starting a business

03Register the business

Apply for an Australian Business Number (ABN) if eligible; the ABN registration itself is free. Register a business name if required, and arrange company or tax registrations as applicable.

Australian Government: Business Registration Service

04Check Brisbane licences and approvals

Check what permits your type of business needs, including industry licences, food licences, signage or planning approvals. Brisbane City Council rules can apply to both commercial premises and home-based businesses.

Queensland Business Launchpad: Find licences and permits

Brisbane City Council: Home-based business requirements

05Prepare your finances and operations

Set up record keeping, banking and invoicing; understand GST and other tax obligations; assess appropriate insurance; and arrange premises, suppliers and staffing if needed.

Business Queensland: Setting up a new business

06Launch and help people find you

Set up your business website, contact details, online presence and marketing. When ready, you can request a free listing in the Business Discovery Network to help Brisbane customers discover your business.

Request a free business listing

Important: This is a general guide, not legal, accounting or licensing advice. Requirements vary by industry, location and business structure. Confirm current requirements with the linked government authorities before trading.
